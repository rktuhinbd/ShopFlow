# Product Overview

## What is ShopFlow?

ShopFlow is a modern, production-oriented native Android e-commerce application focused on product discovery, search, category browsing, product detail, favorites, offline-first access, and a polished adaptive mobile experience.

## Core User Journeys

ShopFlow focuses on the critical initial phases of the e-commerce funnel:

1. **Product Discovery**: Browsing a paginated, performant catalog of products.
2. **Search & Filtering**: Finding specific items via text search or category filtering.
3. **Product Evaluation**: Viewing detailed product information, high-quality images, and specifications on the Product Detail screen.
4. **Curation**: Saving products to a personalized Favorites list for later review.
5. **Offline Browsing**: Accessing previously loaded products seamlessly without an active internet connection.

## Product Philosophy

- **Speed is a Feature**: The app must be instantly responsive.
- **Content is King**: The UI is restrained to let the product photography and details shine.
- **Always Available**: Network flakiness should never break the core browsing experience; hence, the strict offline-first architecture.

## Supported Form Factors

ShopFlow is designed as a responsive application, gracefully adapting its Jetpack Compose UI from standard phone form factors to larger window sizes in landscape or tablet orientations.
